OUR SERVICES

Supporting Our Community with Care

Kwelano Bohloko Old Age and Disability Centre focuses on community support that promotes dignity, inclusion and wellbeing.

Our services and activities are designed around the needs of older persons, people with disabilities and the wider community.

♥

Serving with compassion.

👵 Support for Older Persons

Older members of our community deserve to feel valued, respected and included.

Our community-focused work aims to promote companionship, dignity and meaningful participation among older persons.

  • Promoting dignity and respect
  • Encouraging social interaction
  • Supporting community participation
  • Creating opportunities for companionship

♿ Disability Support

We believe that people with disabilities should be included and respected within the community.

Our approach focuses on encouraging inclusion, participation and community support.

  • Promoting inclusion
  • Encouraging participation
  • Supporting dignity and respect
  • Building community awareness
