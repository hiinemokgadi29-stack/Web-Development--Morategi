ABOUT KWELANO BOHLOKO

Caring for People, Strengthening Our Community

Kwelano Bohloko Old Age and Disability Centre is a community-focused organisation dedicated to supporting older persons and people with disabilities.

We believe that caring for our community means creating an environment where people feel respected, valued, included and supported.

♥

Care begins with compassion.

Who We Are

Kwelano Bohloko is focused on making a positive difference in the lives of older persons and people with disabilities.

Our work is based on the belief that everyone deserves dignity, kindness and an opportunity to participate in their community.

We encourage community members, families, volunteers and organisations to work together to provide meaningful support.

Our Story

Kwelano Bohloko was established to respond to the needs of vulnerable members of the community, particularly older persons and people living with disabilities.

The centre is based in the Mankweng area of Limpopo and serves as a place where community support and care can come together.

As the organisation continues to grow, our goal is to strengthen our community partnerships and create more opportunities for people to receive support and participate in community life.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission Our Vision

To provide support, dignity, care and inclusion for older persons and people with disabilities while strengthening the local community.

To create a caring and supportive community where older persons and people with disabilities can live with dignity, respect and belonging.

Our Values

Our values guide the way we treat people and the way we work with our community.

Value What It Means
Dignity Every person deserves to be treated with respect and dignity.
Compassion We show kindness, understanding and care to people who need support.
Respect We value every individual and recognise their unique experiences and needs.
Inclusion Everyone deserves to feel included and welcomed in the community.
Community Working together helps us build a stronger and more caring community.

Our Community Approach

We believe that supporting older persons and people with disabilities is a responsibility that belongs to the whole community.

Volunteers, families, local businesses, community organisations and individuals can all play a role.

  • Volunteering time and skills
  • Participating in community activities
  • Sharing information about available support
  • Building partnerships
  • Helping vulnerable community members
