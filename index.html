WELCOME TO KWELANO BOHLOKO

A Place of Care, Dignity and Belonging

Kwelano Bohloko Old Age and Disability Centre is a community-focused organisation dedicated to supporting older persons and people with disabilities.

We believe that every person deserves to be treated with kindness, respect and dignity.

Our centre brings people together through care, support and community.
